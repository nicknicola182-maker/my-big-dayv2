# Social Advert Package — My Big Day
**Tagline:** *My Big Day — the planner who knows your traditions.*
**The mechanic:** every ad opens with a question the target couple already argues about at family dinners. Engagement (poll taps, comments, quiz swipes) is the ad — the app is revealed as the one that already knows the answer, and the download is the payoff. Free download always leads; the £6.99 unlock is mentioned softly or not at all.

**Funnel:** Question hook → 3 seconds of "finally, someone gets it" → app reveal (real screens: the faith picker, the package reveal, the QR album) → "My Big Day — free to download. £6.99 once for the full fantasy — no subscription."

## Ready-to-run ad concepts (in brand voice)

### A — Culture-targeted hooks (one tradition per ad set, geo-targeted)
1. **Greek/Cypriot (Reels, 20s).** Hook text: "How many guests is a *small* Cypriot wedding?" VO: "If you said under 200… καλή τύχη, darling." Cut to app guest screen scrolling past 300+. CTA: "My Big Day — the planner that gets it. Free."
2. **South Asian (Reels/TikTok).** "POV: your wedding app just asked about your 'rehearsal dinner'. Babe. We're having a mehndi, a sangeet AND a jaggo." App shows the events toggle screen with all three, pre-ticked. "Finally."
3. **Jewish (carousel).** Card 1: "Name a wedding app that knows what an aufruf is." Card 2: "We'll wait." Card 3: app checklist showing ketubah, chuppah, sheva brachot. Card 4: "Every movement, every custom. Free download."
4. **Muslim (static/stories, serious-hook variant — straight voice, no darling).** "Your nikah may not be legally recognised in the UK on its own. Your wedding app should have told you." App paperwork screen. "Ours does. Free."
5. **Interfaith/blend (poll ad — native poll sticker).** "Whose traditions win the wedding — yours or your partner's?" [Yours] [Theirs] [Both, obviously →] Poll answer routes to the blend screen: "Two traditions, one love story. We weave both."

### B — Universal hooks
6. **The field.** "Getting married in a field? A barn? A football pitch?" Beat. "You don't need to know how. That's my job, darling." App auto-adds marquee, power, loos, permits on screen. (Our single most differentiated demo.)
7. **The price quiz (carousel/quiz).** "Can you price a wedding in 10 seconds?" Swipe: guests? → budget? → *reveal*: "The [Names] Package — €12,000 all in, €55 a head." "Answer 14 little questions. Get your whole wedding back."
8. **The photo question.** "Your guests took 3,000 photos at the last wedding you went to. Where are they now?" QR scan demo → photos landing in the album live. "One code on the tables. Every photo, yours."
9. **The don't-know couple.** "Just engaged and absolutely no idea where to start?" VO: "Gorgeous. Deep breath, champagne open — fourteen little questions and I'll build the whole thing around you two." Landing page petals → reveal screen.
10. **The receipts.** "Deposit due dates, balance dates, who's paid what — or 'it's in my notes app somewhere'?" Payments-coming-up screen. "Scan the invoice. I'll file it, darling."

## Engagement architecture (the "rope them in" machinery)
- **Pinned first comment repeats the question** — comment sections become the focus group ("comment your tradition, I'll tell you what most couples forget").
- **Poll/quiz stickers on every Stories placement** — the tap is the commitment device; answer routes to the matching onboarding question in the store listing deep link.
- **UGC loop:** "show us the tradition your app never heard of" — feeds pack roadmap AND ad creative.
- **Retargeting order:** engaged-but-not-installed → ad 9 (the warm welcome); installed-but-locked → ad 10 or 6 (paid features earning their keep). Never retarget with pressure copy — the brand doesn't beg.

## Guardrails
- One tradition per ad; never a montage that flattens cultures into confetti.
- Cultural reviewer sign-off before any tradition-specific ad airs (see guidelines, open question 5).
- Legal-adjacent hooks (ad 4) always in the straight voice, always accurate per jurisdiction, never fear-mongering beyond fact.
- Store-compliance: no "free" claims next to IAP screenshots without "in-app purchases" disclosure.

## Measurement starting points
Hook rate (3s view/impressions) target >30% for question hooks; poll tap-through >8%; CPI ceiling ~£1.50 UK culture-targeted; day-7 unlock conversion of ad-sourced installs vs organic as the creative truth-teller.
