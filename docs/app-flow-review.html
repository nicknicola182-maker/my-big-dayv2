Wedding App — the flow, for your review

First open → onboarding → populated plan → daily use. Nothing here is built yet; mark up anything you'd change.

1The journey at a glance

What a brand-new couple experiences, from tapping the icon to a plan that already looks like their wedding.

Open the app for the first timeWarm welcome screen. One line on what the app does. No account demanded yet — "Get started" or "I already have a plan" (restore/sign in).
Onboarding — 14 questions, one per screenBig touch targets, progress bar, back button, skippable where safe. Geo pre-fills suggestions; the couple always confirms. Under 3 minutes.
The engine builds the planAnswers + culture pack + country pricing → budget, events, checklist, paperwork, customs, and the app's own vocabulary. A short "building your plan" moment — then the reveal.
The reveal"Here's your wedding so far" — their tradition, their events, a budget allocated across their sections, a checklist anchored to their date. This is the moment that sells the app.
Create account (soft ask)Offered after the reveal, not before: "Save your plan + plan together with your partner." Email or Apple/Google sign-in. Can be skipped; nagged gently later.
Daily use — the five tabsHome · Budget · Checklist · Guests · More. The couple lives here for the next 12–18 months.
The unlock — £14.99, one-offMet naturally when they tap Suppliers, Export, partner invite, or hit the custom-item cap. Never a pop-up ambush.

2First open — the 14 questions

Each question exists to drive something. If it doesn't change the plan, it isn't asked.

#QuestionAnswer typeWhat it drives

Design rule: geo only ever suggests — tradition and country are always confirmed by the couple. Getting someone's tradition wrong on question two is unrecoverable; letting them confirm it costs one tap.

The ten seconds between the last question and the reveal.

PackQ2/Q3 select and tune one of the 8 packs

LawQ4+Q5 select the paperwork list and legal gotchas
VoicePack terms swap the UI's vocabulary — priest/rabbi/imam/registrar
PrioritiesQ12 rebalances allocation within the total

4The main app — five tabs

Everything from the current v20 app survives; it's re-plumbed to sit on the engine instead of hard-coded Greek Cypriot content.

Home Free

Countdown, budget health at a glance, next 3 checklist tasks, date warnings. The screen they open every time.

Budget Free

Sections and items from the pack, figures from the engine. Track estimated vs agreed vs paid, per item — the heart of the current app, kept.

Checklist Free

Timeline tasks counted back from the date, critical ones flagged. Paperwork lives here as its own filter — the legal must-dos.

Guests Free

Count, sides, RSVPs. Feeds per-guest budget lines live — change the number, watch catering move.

More — Traditions & customs Free

The pack's customs and gotchas as a warm reference: what each tradition means, what catches couples out.

More — Suppliers & enquiries £14.99

Directory filtered to their country and categories, plus the enquiry sender. Browsing shows a teaser; contacting needs the unlock.

More — Partner sync £14.99

Both phones, one plan, live. Prompted at Q14 and from Home. Rides on the cloud account.

More — Export £14.99

PDF summary and Excel budget — for parents, planners and venues.

7Open decisions — your call before I build

D1 — Mixed-tradition couples

Q2 offers "we're mixing two traditions". Simplest honest version for launch: pick a primary pack (drives budget and timeline), and the second tradition's customs and key events are available to toggle on. A full two-pack merge is a later release. Agree?

D2 — Skippable budget (Q8)

If they skip the budget, the app shows proportions ("Reception ~45%") instead of figures until they set one. Keeps the reveal working without forcing a number on day one. Agree?

D3 — Custom item cap for free tier

Proposed: 10 custom budget items and 10 photos free, unlimited with the unlock. Say the word if you want a different number.

D4 — Account timing

Account is offered after the reveal and skippable, so the first-run magic is never blocked by a sign-up form. Cloud sync then rides on the account whenever they create it. Agree?

D5 — The reveal screen

One screen or a short guided tour (3 swipes: your budget → your checklist → your traditions)? I lean one strong screen with the tabs doing the touring. Your preference?
