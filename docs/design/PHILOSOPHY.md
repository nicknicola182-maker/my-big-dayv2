# Champagne Maximalism
*A design philosophy*

Champagne Maximalism is the aesthetics of the held breath before the kiss — joy engineered with couture discipline. It believes celebration is not decoration but structure: every surface warm, every motion buoyant, every colour chosen the way a florist chooses stems at five in the morning. It is exuberance executed with the painstaking care of a master atelier — nothing accidental, everything alive. The work must read as though it took countless hours, because it did; the seams are hand-finished even where no one looks.

**Space and form.** Generosity is the grid. Content floats centred in abundant air, like a single tiered cake on a long linen table. Forms are soft-cornered and confident — petals, arches, rings — never boxes for their own sake. The eye is escorted, one delight at a time, down the centre line of the page. Emptiness is not absence; it is anticipation.

**Colour and material.** A base of warm ivory and blush like late-afternoon light on silk, then unapologetic strikes of colour: rose deepening to raspberry, apricot gold, a surprise of pistachio or Aegean blue. Gradients behave like champagne — pale at the rim, warmer at the heart. Gold is a seasoning, never a sauce. Everything glows slightly, as if lit by candles and good news.

**Scale and rhythm.** Display type is tall, romantic, high-contrast — a serif with a waist — while working text is a clean, rounded sans that stays out of the way. Scale swings are theatrical: one enormous loving word, then a whisper of instruction. Motion is petals and confetti, floated not thrown: slow drifts, gentle rises, a shimmer of arrival on every screen. Nothing bounces frantically; everything lands like a dancer.

**Composition and hierarchy.** Each screen is a toast — one sentiment, raised high, perfectly poured. The primary action blooms at the centre; secondary paths curtsey at the edges. Voice is part of the visual system: words are warm, personal, slightly outrageous, delivered like the most in-demand planner in the city taking your hands in theirs. The craft standard is absolute: alignments rehearsed to the pixel, colour calibrated until it hums, the whole surface polished with the reverence of a couturier pressing a final hem. Joy, meticulously made.
