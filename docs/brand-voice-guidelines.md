## 1. Brand essence

**One line:** My Big Day — the wedding planner who actually knows your traditions, and is beside herself with excitement that you called.

**Name logic:** *My* Big Day — possessive, personal, the couple's own. Every competitor says "the big day"; ours belongs to someone.

**The persona.** The brand speaks as one character: a top-tier, larger-than-life, fabulous wedding planner. She calls you *darling* in the first minute and means it. She has planned a nikah in Bradford, a baraat in Leicester, a stefana crowning in Limassol and a humanist do in a field, and she loved every one. The sparkle is the surface; the substance underneath is encyclopaedic competence — she knows the Metropolis needs your freedom-to-marry certificate, that celebrant ceremonies aren't legally binding in England, and that your deposit is due Thursday.

**The moat (why this voice exists).** Every competitor is a generic checklist in wedding-white. Bridebook is free and dominant but culturally blank. We are the only app where a Hindu couple, a Jewish couple and a Cypriot couple each feel the app was built for *them*. The voice carries that promise: intimate, specific, never one-size-fits-all.

## 3. Voice principles (with canonical examples from the app)

1. **Address the couple like your favourite clients.** "Introduce me to the happy couple." / "How many hearts in the room?" Never "users", never "enter guest count".
2. **Sparkle first, then substance — in the same breath.** "Numbers, darling. Talk to me. — No judgement here, a budget is just ambition with a plan."
3. **Competence is the punchline.** "A farm, a football pitch, a field with a view — you don't need to know how, darling. That's my job." Then it *actually adds the marquee, power, loos and permits to the plan.* Every flourish must cash a real cheque.
4. **Every tradition is the main character.** "I know every one of these inside out — and I adore them all equally." Real terms, correctly used: mehndi, sangeet, ketubah, aufruf, nikah, walima, stefana, koumbaroi, Anand Karaj. Italicise nothing, exoticise nothing, explain generously.
5. **Warnings drop the act.** Legal and money warnings are delivered clean: "An unlicensed site usually means a separate signing." No darling, no wink. The contrast is the trust signal.
6. **Generous, never grasping.** The paywall reads like a gift list, not a ransom note: "The full fantasy — once, £6.99. No subscription. Yours forever." Never fake urgency, never countdown timers, never guilt.

## 4. Lexicon

**Words we own:** darling / darlings · gorgeous · celebrations (not "events") · "hearts in the room" · "the full fantasy" · "your plan" (not "the app") · "react to it, don't obey it" · "that's my job".
**Words we never use:** bridezilla · exotic · ethnic (as a noun) · "simple/easy" about anyone's tradition · "dream wedding" (cliché cap: sparingly). NOTE: "big day" is now the brand — use it as a proper noun (My Big Day), and sparingly as a phrase so the name keeps its shine · users · monetise-scented words in couple-facing copy (unlock is "the full fantasy", not "premium tier").
**Grammar of the voice:** short declaratives, theatrical dashes, one endearment per screen maximum. She's effusive, not breathless.

**Type** (all Google Fonts — usable in any ad tool): **Gloock** for display ("the romance"), **Outfit** for everything working ("the competence"), **Nothing You Could Do** for handwritten asides only — one per composition, always lowercase, always the planner's whisper ("darling…", "built just for you…").
**Motifs:** drifting petals, gold sparkle dots, letterspaced-caps eyebrows (OUR WEDDING), soft tinted panels, colour-topped stat cards, dates written out in words.
**Photography for ads:** golden-hour, candid, mid-laugh, real ritual detail (mehndi hands, the glass underfoot, crowns exchanged, dhol drummers) — never posed stock, never all-white-wedding-only casting.
**Logo direction (open):** wordmark in Gloock with a script ampersand or petal mark; avoid rings/hearts clichés.

## 8. Open questions

1. **The name — RESOLVED: My Big Day.** Chosen 30 Jul 2026. Applied across app, landing, guest album page. Remaining: formal UK IPO trademark search (crowded neighbourhood: "The Big Day", "thebigdays", "Your Big Day" all active), domain + handle registrations (mybigday.app etc.).
2. **Paid tier name.** Recommend "The Full Fantasy — £6.99, once" as consumer-facing label for the unlock.
3. **Who fronts video ads?** The persona implies a face/voice. Options: founder-adjacent talent, drag/queer talent consistent with the voice's DNA (authentic, not costume — see We Are Not), or animated/voiceover only. Decision needed before video production.
4. **Ad market order.** Recommend geo-and-culture-targeted ad sets (one tradition per ad, per city) rather than generic launch ads — the moat is specificity.
5. **Cultural sign-off process.** Before any tradition-specific ad airs, one reviewer from that community approves it. Non-negotiable guardrail; who are the reviewers?
